
CANDIDATE INSTRUCTIONS (mandatory). Goal: a strong resume that Workday's parser reads cleanly and auto-fills into Work Experience, Education and Skills.
1. Write the ENTIRE resume in English. Languages: Portuguese (native), English (fluent), Spanish (fluent).
2. WORKDAY FORMAT: use exactly these section headings, in this order, as plain "## " headings with no bold: Summary, Skills, Work Experience, Education, Certifications, Languages. Name as "# " heading, then ONE plain contact line (email | phone | LinkedIn | city, state). No HTML, tables, columns, icons, text boxes or bold in headings. Each role as "### Job Title | Company" (no bold), then one line exactly "MM/YYYY - MM/YYYY | City, Country" using two-digit months (use "Present" for the current role). Every role MUST have a date line; a role without dates in the master (Rivool) becomes a bullet under Summary, not a separate role. Education lines as "Degree, Field - Institution (YYYY - YYYY)". Plain "-" bullets, one sentence each, action verb first, past tense for ended roles.
3. KEYWORDS: naturally include the job's exact terms where the master supports them, and build a Skills list of 12-16 short items mirroring the job's vocabulary.
4. TRUTH: use ONLY facts in the master resume. Job requirements guide emphasis and wording; they are not facts about the candidate. Do not invent metrics, team sizes, dates, budgets, channels, partners or experience. Reverse-chronological order. Two pages maximum.
